CMSM privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026.

CMSM is operated for the owner of the connected Google Drive account. This policy describes its storage and authorization behavior; it does not change Google's own terms or privacy policy.

Information accessed and stored

CMSM reads local files under the owner's configured roots when they are selected for a supported archive or restoration operation. It records file content, machine and source-path metadata, file size, timestamps, checksums, transfer checkpoints and verification results as needed to preserve and verify an archive.

The app uses the Google Drive drive.file scope. It creates archive objects and manifests in the owner's Drive and reads authorized archive objects back for verification and restoration. This scope covers files created by CMSM and files explicitly authorized for the app; it does not permit unrestricted discovery of all Drive files.

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in a dedicated local configuration on the Mac controller. The Desktop client JSON is supplied locally by the account owner. The Windows agent does not receive Google OAuth credentials. Credentials are excluded from CMSM status responses and acceptance evidence.

Purposes and sharing

File content and metadata are used for archiving, integrity checking, restoration, storage monitoring and owner-requested troubleshooting. Archive bytes are sent to the owner's Google Drive and read back to the controller for verification. They may be restored to a destination the owner chooses.

CMSM does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. The runtime is a deterministic storage program rather than an AI agent with access to the Google account.

When the owner authorizes technical diagnostics through a private development environment, reports may include machine status, source paths, file sizes, checksums and synthetic acceptance results. Credential values are excluded. These diagnostics are separate from publication of these informational pages, which contain no archive contents, tokens or machine-specific paths.

Retention and removal

CMSM exposes no operation for deleting or pruning cloud archives. Market raw datasets receive the PROTECTED_FOREVER retention policy. This is an application policy, not a claim that Google Drive provides immutable storage; the account owner or another authorized application can still affect Drive files.

The current candidate keeps local removal disabled. A future enabled archive operation may remove a local copy only after current inactivity/dependency checks and complete remote verification pass. Cleanup review does not perform automatic permanent deletion.

Revoking authorization stops future access; it does not delete archives already stored in the owner's Drive. The owner remains responsible for the connected Google account, storage capacity and other applications that can access it.

Control and contact

The owner can stop CMSM, review its configuration, and revoke its Google authorization from the Google Account's third-party connections settings. The app's Google consent screen identifies its developer/support contact.

Material changes to data use or access will require an update to this policy and any necessary authorization changes.
