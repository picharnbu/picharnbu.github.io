CMSM terms of use

Last updated: 8 October 2026.

Intended use

CMSM is a personal storage utility for files and a Google Drive account the operator is authorized to use. It is not currently offered as a public multi-user storage service.

The operator supplies a Desktop OAuth client for the application and grants the limited drive.file permission through Google's own consent screen. This website does not collect account passwords, authorization codes or client JSON files.

Operating conditions

The Mac is the sole scheduler and cloud-transfer controller. The Windows agent serves configured local sources only. Ordinary heavy archive work is scheduled for 23:00–05:00 Asia/Bangkok. Emergency work remains subject to the same data-safety checks.

The 75 GB free-space target depends on available safe archive candidates, account capacity, connectivity and producer/dependency evidence. It is a recovery target rather than a guaranteed amount of free space at every moment.

The operator must keep required Google account/storage access available and avoid conflicting changes by other storage tools during a reviewed cutover. Google account and service terms continue to apply.

Data safety and release status

The app verifies source and destination size, SHA-256, readability, transfer completeness and its manifest before a local copy becomes eligible for removal. Active, required or insufficiently understood data is retained. Market raw data remains permanently protected by the archive policy. CMSM offers no cloud-delete or cloud-prune operation, and cleanup reports require human review.

The current version 0.1.0 remains a candidate with production transfer and local removal disabled. A successful synthetic cloud test is not authorization to remove production datasets. Dataset contracts, authorization durability, controller reload, legacy scheduler cutover and production acceptance remain required before enabling unattended offload.

An app-level permanent-retention policy cannot prevent manual deletion or changes by another application with access to Google Drive. Review backups and restoration evidence before depending on them for recovery.

Contact and changes

The application's Google consent screen identifies its developer/support contact. Changes to the app's intended use, permissions or data practices will be reflected in these pages.
